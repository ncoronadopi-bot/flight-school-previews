How an audit runs here

One request list, at the start

Before fieldwork we send a single list of everything we need, with due dates, in English or Spanish.

Fieldwork tests what you sent

We confirm balances with your bank and customers, test samples of transactions and walk through the controls behind them.

A draft before we sign

You read the draft statements and our management letter first. Then we issue the signed report.

Services, in full

The audit is the centre of the practice. Around it sit the books, the controls and the tax work that make an audit go smoothly.

  • Audits, reviews, compilations

    Financial statements at the level your bank, investors or regulator ask for.

  • Bookkeeping and controllership

    Monthly closes and reconciliations, so year-end has no surprises.

  • Due diligence

    Acquisition planning and investigations before you buy a business.

  • Internal control reviews

    Testing and documentation of the controls behind your numbers.

  • Accounting system setup

    Charts of accounts and software for new businesses. QuickBooks ProAdvisor.

  • International tax

    US tax and reporting for foreign investors and international corporations.

  • Outsourced internal audit

    A full internal audit function, reporting to your board or audit committee.

  • Forecasts and projections

    Prospective statements for lenders, investors and your own planning.

  • Tax and IRS representation

    Business returns, and representation before the IRS and state authorities.

  • Operational audits

    Quality assurance reviews and operational audits of how work gets done.

  • Strategic planning

    Analysis and planning for start-ups and growing companies.

  • Payroll

    Online payroll and payroll taxes for small and midsize businesses.

About the firm

We have been in practice since 1998, first as Guernica & Gonzalez, CPA and Consultants, and now as Guernica & Associates.

Our work is national and international: corporate tax, advisory and assurance for national firms, captive foreign insurance companies and international investors, and for small and midsize companies here in South Florida.

Our professionals work in English and Spanish, so owners and their staff can talk through their numbers in the language they think in.

We treat each client relationship as a partnership. When your statements hold up in front of your bank, your board or your regulator, we have done our job.
